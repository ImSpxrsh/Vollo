# Free alternatives for VOLL's art

Similar free assets for each kind of sprite in the game, to replace images found through Google or to credit
them properly. Licences are as listed on each page when searched (Nov 2026). **Check the licence on the page
before you download**, because creators can change them.

- **CC0** = public domain, no credit needed (crediting is still nice)
- **CC BY** = free, but you must credit the artist
- **Custom / unconfirmed** = read the page first

## If you keep an image you found on Google

Run it through a reverse image search ([Google Lens](https://lens.google.com) or [TinEye](https://tineye.com)) to
find the original artist and licence, then credit them in `godot/scenes/credits/credits_label.tscn` and on the
itch page. If the licence doesn't allow reuse, swap it for one of the assets above.
